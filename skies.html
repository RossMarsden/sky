Paths and sky guide

Gold: Sun · pale blue: Moon · peach: Venus. Only these three have daily paths and define the horizon span. Mars, Jupiter and Saturn show current positions only. Saturn’s rings and Jupiter’s four moon dots are symbolic.

Dashed gold: Sun limits. Dashed blue: Moon major limits ±28.6°; dotted blue: minor limits ±18.3°. Green dots: celestial equator. The pale violet Milky Way sketch is fixed against the stars; its widening is illustrative, not a measured brightness boundary. It stops at the last point above the horizon.

The reflected sky above shares the zenith point. Mollweide distorts angles and distances; 10° at the horizon matches the height from 0° to 10° altitude.

Mollweide sky · horizon below, shared zenith point, reflected southern sky above. Azimuth curves converge at the zenith; altitude lines remain horizontal. Horizontally stretched so a 10° horizon interval matches the height from 0° to 10° altitude. The horizon spans today’s outermost rise and set bearings, plus 5°.
